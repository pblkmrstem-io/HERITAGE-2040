Future Ideas

Practical ideas for protecting, promoting, and sharing Perak's heritage in 2040 through digital technology, sustainability, education, and community participation.

01 // Smart Heritage Mapping

An intelligent digital mapping system connecting Perak's heritage sites, historical information, visitor routes, and preservation records in one accessible platform.

02 // AI Heritage Guide

An AI-powered educational assistant that helps people learn about Perak's heritage and directs them towards reliable and verified historical sources.

03 // Eco-Smart Preservation

Sustainable practices and environmental monitoring that could help reduce the impact of climate, tourism, and human activity on heritage sites.

04 // Heritage Cloud Archive

A digital archive for preserving photographs, historical documents, oral histories, research, and verified heritage information for future generations.

05 // Youth Heritage Network

A platform that encourages students and young people to research, document, and promote local heritage through digital storytelling and community projects.

06 // Smart Heritage Management

A future system that helps heritage organisations organise information, manage visitors, and support conservation planning and decision-making.
